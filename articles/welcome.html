Welcome to Literary Comet

MeteorDream·Published 2024-03-15·Updated 2025-04-20·114 words·metahello

Why this site exists

Most of what passes for "content" online is written to be scanned, not read. This corner of the web is for the opposite: short essays, half-thoughts, and margin notes that reward a slower pace.

If something here makes you close the tab and stare out the window, that's a feature.

What to expect

  • Essays on books, language, and the small mechanics of style.
  • Occasional long-form pieces on craft and revision.
  • Notes that may never become anything more than notes.

A note on frequency

There is no posting schedule. There never will be.

Get in touch

The footer has the usual handles. Email is best for anything longer than a sentence.
